
EstateX Product Requirement Document (PRD)

Marketing to Booking

Version: 2.0

Status: Draft

Founder: Akshay Chandekar

Product: EstateX

Category: AI Business Operating System (AI-BOS)

Executive Summary

Vision
Build the world's first AI Business Operating System for Real Estate Developers that manages the complete customer journey from Marketing to Booking.

EstateX transforms sales operations by replacing repetitive manual work with intelligent AI employees that work 24×7.

Mission
Help every real estate developer increase bookings while reducing operational costs using an AI-powered workforce.

Problem Statement
Today's developers spend millions on marketing but continue losing customers because:

* Leads are not contacted instantly

* Follow-ups stop after a few attempts

* Site visits are not managed efficiently

* Sales teams are inconsistent

* Managers lack real-time visibility

* CRMs store information but don't actively drive sales

The result is poor lead conversion and unnecessary operational costs.

Solution
EstateX is an AI Revenue Operating System.

Instead of acting like a CRM, EstateX behaves like an intelligent sales organization.

Every lead is handled by an AI Workforce that:

Responds instantly

Qualifies customers

Schedules site visits

Sends reminders

Follows up automatically

Predicts booking probability

Assists managers with recommendations

Updates CRM automatically

Product Philosophy
Traditional CRM

Store customer information.

EstateX

Actively generate bookings.

Core Principles
Every feature must satisfy at least one of the following:

Increase bookings

Reduce operational cost

Save employee time

Improve customer experience

Provide measurable business insights

If a feature doesn't satisfy these principles, it doesn't belong in the MVP.

Target Customers
Primary

Medium and large real estate developers generating between 200 and 10,000 leads per month.

Secondary

Channel Partners

Real Estate Brokers

Sales Agencies

Success Metrics
Within six months of implementation, EstateX should help developers achieve:

95% first response within one minute

80% automated follow-up coverage

20% increase in site visits

10–20% improvement in bookings

40% reduction in manual sales operations

Company Vision
We are not building another CRM.

We are building the operating system for real estate sales.

Product Positioning
Instead of saying

"AI Calling Software"

EstateX will be positioned as

"Your AI Workforce for Real Estate."

Brand Promise
Never lose another customer because of poor follow-up.
